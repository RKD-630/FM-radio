How to Use Audio Cutter Pro

Importing: Click "Choose File" or drop an audio/video file.

Selecting & Cutting: Click and drag on the waveform to select a section of audio. Click the Cut button (scissors) to delete the selected part, or the Trim button to keep ONLY the selection and remove everything else.

Splitting: Move the playhead to any position and click Split. This breaks the track into smaller segments shown in the list. You can select and delete individual segments.

Zooming: Use the zoom controls under the waveform or pinch to zoom in for precise editing.

Saving MP3: When you're done, scroll down to Export Settings. Enter a file name, select MP3 as the format, choose your desired quality (bitrate), and click Export Audio or Save at the bottom. The file will be processed and downloaded automatically.
